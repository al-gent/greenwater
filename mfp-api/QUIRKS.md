# MFP data quirks

Things UNOLS Marine Facilities Planning does that don't make much sense from where we sit,
and what we do about each. Companion to `README.md` (endpoints, fields). Add to this whenever
the data surprises you; it's the argument for why VesselConnect's model is not MFP's.

| # | What MFP does | Why it bites | What we do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Schedules applications, not sailings.** Each funded project is its own "cruise" with its own ids; co-scheduled projects just get identical dates on the same vessel. No shared key. | One vessel shows 2–6 simultaneous cruises. 199 sailings / 279 extra rows in the snapshot, one row in six. | `cruise_projects` one-to-one with applications; cruises built by grouping on vessel + dates (`20260930_cruise_projects.sql`). |
| 2 | **"Total Science Party Size" is inconsistent.** On the SME form it is sometimes the whole cruise's party, sometimes the project's own headcount, mixed even within one sailing (Thompson TN-422: 1, 2, 1, 36, 36, 1). | Summing across projects triple-counts (Sally Ride: 63 on a 26-berth ship). | Cruise headcount = max across its projects. Treat as "at least". |
| 3 | **The same project can be filed twice** by different requesters (CalCOFI Fall 2026 under two applications, same title, same dates). | Looks like a duplicate; is two awards. | Both stay as projects on one cruise. |
| 4 | **Four ids per application** (`CruiseApplication_id`, `Workflow_id`, `Cruise_id`, `VoyageEvent_id`) and none is shared by co-scheduled applications. `Cruise_id` does not mean "the sailing". | Nothing to join on for grouping. | Group on vessel + departure + arrival. |
| 5 | **No status field.** Confirmed / tentative / cancelled is inferred from funding status, `ActualDeparture`, number of ships, and whether an operator cruise number exists. | Status is a heuristic ladder we maintain. | `derived_status` on the project; cruise takes the firmest. Once operators author, stage is ours. |
| 6 | **Operator cruise number is optional and non-unique.** `AlternateReferenceNumber` missing on 31% of rows, repeated on 15% (FLOTSUM ×8). | Can't be the URL or a key. | Slugs from vessel + code-or-title + month. But when present it is the best signal of "the sailing", so it picks the merge survivor. |
| 7 | **Two date formats in one record.** ISO on legs, `M/D/YYYY` on `Cruise.Start/End`. | Parsing. | `toIso()` handles both. |
| 8 | **Dates are calendar days with no time or zone.** | A cruise "departs March 3" is a date, not an instant. | Stored as UTC midnight, `all_day = true` on timeline entries. |
| 9 | **Vessel is a name.** The application's parked vessel is `Cruise.Start.Voyage.FlexLocation.Name`; no id or MMSI on the record. | Name matching. | Resolve to our vessel id at import via the MFP vessel list (MMSI first, then name); never store the name. |
| 10 | **Planner geometry includes the trip from home port** (`ShipTransect` often starts at the operator's base, not the cruise's departure port). | Map fit and steaming-time sums are wrong if taken literally. | Routes drawn faint, excluded from fit; ports from legs win over planner "Port" points. |
| 11 | **`BoundCoordinates` is a two-corner "polygon".** | Leaflet draws a diagonal. | Not drawn; it only restates the features' extent. |
| 12 | **Ship-fitted equipment and requested gear are only in an HTML form** (the SME pane), not in any JSON endpoint. | Had to harvest and parse HTML per cruise. | `harvest_mfp_sme.mjs`, cached on disk; 1,721 of 1,723 parsed. |
| 13 | **A duplicated test record** in the public schedule feed. | — | Dropped on `CruiseApplication_id`. |
| 14 | **Applications are parked with identical dates even when one is clearly a piggyback** and another a real second departure (Savannah: a 2-day project inside a 10-day one). | Can't tell rotation-at-port from co-scheduling. | Identical windows merge (M1). Contained windows merge as piggyback projects (`scripts/fixes/20261001_merge_contained_piggybacks.sql`, 10 rows). Staggered overlaps stay separate: mostly same-day turnarounds, which the timeline packs on one row. |
| 15 | **`PooledEquipment` means different things per operator.** UAF flags all 211 Sikuliaq items pooled, UW all 52; SkIO, UH, UMN, BIOS, UMIAMI flag none. | The flag is not "shared across the fleet". | Not stored. A unit is shared if `equipment_locations` shows more than one vessel; it is a timeline resource if a cruise assigns it. |
| 16 | **The national deep-submergence assets are not in the public inventory.** No Jason, Alvin, Sentry, or any ROV/AUV/HOV rows; the only "vehicles" are towed bodies, moored profilers and drop cameras. | The gear most worth scheduling is missing. | Add NDSF vehicles by hand as pooled units under WHOI. |
| 17 | **Test records ship in the public schedule feed** ("TEST SME to review SKQ PM Workflow" on Langseth, "TEST" on Narragansett Dawn), and one carries a mobilization date **three years before departure**. Two more real cruises have mob/demob 3–5 weeks away from the cruise. | A year-long bar on the timeline; occupancy windows that swallow other cruises. | Both test records unpublished (2026-09-30). `cruiseOccupancy()` ignores mob/demob more than 14 days from the cruise. |
| 18 | **Leg arrivals carry a time of day; mob/demob are dates.** `DemobilisationDate` is midnight on the arrival day, while the last leg's `Arrival` is e.g. 16:00 that day, so demob < arrival by hours on 390 of 1,434 live cruises. Two cruises have a mobilization dated after departure. | A guard that compared timestamps naively thought demob was "before arrival". | `cruiseOccupancy()` honours mob only if ≤ departure and demob only if ≥ arrival (and within 14 days); all-day rendering snaps to day boundaries so the hours never show. `scripts/fixes/20261013_occupancy_guard.sql` re-derived 392 entries (visually unchanged, now consistent). |
